1. English (EN)

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how PrimeVocab ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares your data when you use the PrimeVocab browser extension and mobile application.

  • Data Collection & Verification: We collect your email and active license key during purchase via Lemon Squeezy to verify your Premium status. We generate an anonymous Device UUID to enforce our 3-device limit per license. We do not sell or share this data with third parties.
  • Google Drive Access (Limited Scope): If you choose to enable the optional cloud synchronization feature, PrimeVocab requests access to your Google Drive. We only request and access the appDataFolder scope. This is a secure, isolated folder that belongs solely to PrimeVocab. We use this access exclusively to backup and synchronize your personal vocabulary lists and settings. We do not and cannot access any other personal files, photos, or documents in your Google Drive.
  • Google API Limited Use Disclosure:

PrimeVocab’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • Data Storage: All personal vocabulary data and study history are stored locally on your device (browser local storage). License validation logs are stored securely on our Google Apps Script servers.
  • Data Deletion: You can clear your local vocabulary data at any time via the Settings panel. To delete your sync data, simply unlink your Google account or delete the app data folder via your Google Drive settings.

Terms of Service

  • License Terms: Premium licenses are personal, non-transferable, and can be active on up to three (3) devices simultaneously.
  • Refund Policy: Payments are processed via Lemon Squeezy. We offer a 14-day refund window. Contact: primevocabextension@gmail.com
